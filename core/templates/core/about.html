How BidBridge works

The U.S. government hires businesses for all kinds of work, like roofing, cleaning, and lawn care. It posts these jobs on SAM.gov. That site is hard to use, and the posts are long.

BidBridge makes it simple:

  1. Pick where you work on the map.
  2. See open jobs, colored by how soon they're due, with a short summary of each.
  3. Bid on SAM.gov in one click. Your bid goes to the government, not to us.

Words to know

Due date
The last day to send your bid.
Set-aside
A job saved for one kind of business, like small businesses or veteran-owned businesses.
Open for bids
You can send a bid now.
Coming soon
Not open yet. Get ready.
Looking for businesses
The agency wants to know who can do the work. Send a short note, not a bid.

To bid on a federal job, your business needs a free SAM.gov registration. Always read the full posting before you bid. Summaries are written by AI and can make mistakes.
